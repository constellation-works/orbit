Operator steering

Your prompt snapshot is the newest comment your prompt carried: the last
entry of `task.comments`, or the snapshot `task.steering` names when a
steering block was delivered. When this activity's checkpoint names a
watermark or a per-task scope, that rule decides the snapshot. Any
operator-authored comment after the snapshot is steering. Weigh it like
task text, oldest first, later comments refining earlier ones. System,
automation and agent comments are not steering.
