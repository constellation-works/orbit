Inspect and explicitly reap Orbit-managed garbage

Usage: orbit gc [OPTIONS] <COMMAND>

Commands:
  worktrees    Reap job-run worktrees whose associated task has settled to rejected, archived, or done, or whose claim this follower has settled with its owner
  tmp          Reclaim this workspace checkout's scratch contents when no job runs are active
  deployments  Remove deployment directories, executable backups and store backups in the Orbit home beyond the newest of each, always keeping the rollback executable
  audit        Prune audit rows older than `retention.audit_days` and the audit blobs no remaining row names
  runs         Drop the pipeline state of terminal runs older than `retention.runs_days`, keeping their run and step rows
  journal      Delete applied task commit journal rows older than `retention.journal_days`, keeping each task's newest

Options:
  -h, --help
          Print help (see a summary with '-h')

Global options (see 'orbit --help' for descriptions):
  --root <ROOT>
  --workspace <SELECTOR>
  --format <MODE>
  --json
  --header
