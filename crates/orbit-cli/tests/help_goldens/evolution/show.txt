Show an evolution record and its occurrences

Usage: orbit evolution show [OPTIONS] <ID>

Arguments:
  <ID>
          Evolution record identifier

Options:
  -h, --help
          Print help (see a summary with '-h')

Global options (see 'orbit --help' for descriptions):
  --root <ROOT>
  --workspace <SELECTOR>
  --format <MODE>
  --json
  --header
