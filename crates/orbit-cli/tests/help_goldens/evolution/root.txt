Record and inspect capability gaps and their measured costs

Usage: orbit evolution [OPTIONS] <COMMAND>

Commands:
  add      Record an interactive cost event
  list     List evolution signatures and summed costs
  show     Show an evolution record and its occurrences
  decline  Suppress proposals for a signature (operator only)

Options:
  -h, --help
          Print help (see a summary with '-h')

Global options (see 'orbit --help' for descriptions):
  --root <ROOT>
  --workspace <SELECTOR>
  --format <MODE>
  --json
  --header
