List evolution signatures and summed costs

Usage: orbit evolution list [OPTIONS]

Options:
      --state <STATE>
          Filter open, proposed, declined or resolved

      --since <SINCE>
          Aggregate occurrences since this RFC 3339 instant

  -h, --help
          Print help (see a summary with '-h')

Global options (see 'orbit --help' for descriptions):
  --root <ROOT>
  --workspace <SELECTOR>
  --format <MODE>
  --json
  --header
