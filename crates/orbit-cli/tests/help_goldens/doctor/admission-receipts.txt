Show admission receipt counts and logical payload bytes, with separate claim-mutation receipt usage

Usage: orbit doctor admission-receipts [OPTIONS]

Options:
      --compact
          Compact idle and settled admission receipts to permanent tombstones; keep unsettled claims' full receipts

  -h, --help
          Print help (see a summary with '-h')

Global options (see 'orbit --help' for descriptions):
  --root <ROOT>
  --workspace <SELECTOR>
  --format <MODE>
  --json
  --header
