Show a condition's matches, closest misses and clause tree over live state, or a job's auto-task steps

Usage: orbit automation explain [OPTIONS] <DEFINITION>

Arguments:
  <DEFINITION>
          Routine or auto-task name (routine:<name> or auto-task:<name> when both exist), or job:<name> for a job's auto-task steps

Options:
      --subject <SUBJECT>
          Subject whose clause tree to show (a task ID, or workspace)

  -h, --help
          Print help (see a summary with '-h')

Global options (see 'orbit --help' for descriptions):
  --root <ROOT>
  --workspace <SELECTOR>
  --format <MODE>
  --json
  --header

Explain reads the task store, the drain's eligibility view and open runs, and writes
nothing. A task-scope condition is evaluated over the tasks its status or tag prefilter
selects, at most 200 per explain. Conditions do not fire yet: arm state, holds, fires
and `self` values are shown as not yet tracked. job:<name> shows a job's auto-task steps
as checked at load, with each named auto-task resolved; such jobs cannot run yet.
Examples:
  orbit automation explain release-prep
  orbit automation explain routine:release-on-prep-done --subject <task-id> --json
  orbit automation explain job:release-readiness
