Explain condition-triggered routines and auto-tasks and jobs' auto-task steps, and clear their stalls

Usage: orbit automation [OPTIONS] <COMMAND>

Commands:
  explain      Show a condition's matches, closest misses and clause tree over live state, or a job's auto-task steps
  clear-stall  Clear a stalled condition on the workspace owner, audited

Options:
  -h, --help
          Print help (see a summary with '-h')

Global options (see 'orbit --help' for descriptions):
  --root <ROOT>
  --workspace <SELECTOR>
  --format <MODE>
  --json
  --header
