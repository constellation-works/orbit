Clear a stalled condition on the workspace owner, audited

Usage: orbit automation clear-stall [OPTIONS] --reason <REASON> <DEFINITION>

Arguments:
  <DEFINITION>
          Routine or auto-task name (routine:<name> or auto-task:<name> when both exist)

Options:
      --reason <REASON>
          Why the stall is cleared; kept in the audit record

  -h, --help
          Print help (see a summary with '-h')

Global options (see 'orbit --help' for descriptions):
  --root <ROOT>
  --workspace <SELECTOR>
  --format <MODE>
  --json
  --header

Operator only. The stall is cleared under the condition state's generation fence
and the audit record is written in the same transaction. Nothing fires here: the next
clock pass evaluates the condition, and a cause that persists stalls it again.
Examples:
  orbit automation clear-stall release-prep --reason "storm was a bulk import"
