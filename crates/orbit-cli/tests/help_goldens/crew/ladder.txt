Show each tier's pool, floor, candidate rung and shadow trial

Usage: orbit crew ladder [OPTIONS]

Options:
  -h, --help
          Print help (see a summary with '-h')

Global options (see 'orbit --help' for descriptions):
  --root <ROOT>
  --workspace <SELECTOR>
  --format <MODE>
  --json
  --header

The floor is a tier's cheapest rung of workflow.crew_ladder.rungs among the enabled,
weighted members of workflow.<tier>_complexity_crews; the candidate is the rung directly
below it. With workflow.crew_ladder.mode = "shadow", each listed tier with a candidate
keeps one shadow experiment owned by system:crew-ladder: its tasks are assigned to the
control or candidate arm and recorded, and no task's crew changes. WOULD-BE counts the
tasks assigned to the candidate arm. Trials are created, started and concluded at
delivery admission; this command only reads them.
