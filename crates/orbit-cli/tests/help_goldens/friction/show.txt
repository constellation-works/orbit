Show a single friction record

Usage: orbit friction show [OPTIONS] <ID>

Arguments:
  <ID>
          Friction record ID, e.g. FYYYY-MM-NNN

Options:
  -h, --help
          Print help (see a summary with '-h')

Global options (see 'orbit --help' for descriptions):
  --root <ROOT>
  --workspace <SELECTOR>
  --format <MODE>
  --json
  --header
