Rank implementer models by quality and reliability, in tie bands.

One row per model, its crews and effort levels pooled. Each measure (escaped regressions, review outcome, implementer CI failures, implementer-fault final recoveries, crew-caused rework, tool-call errors) is compared with what the fleet achieves on the same task tiers, divided by the season's noise scale, weighted, and shrunk toward the other eligible models. Measures Orbit does not record yet are shown as unmeasured and leave the weights.

The ranked window (--window, 30 days) ends --settle-lag (14 days) before --until, so late escaped regressions have arrived; --live ends it at --until instead and is provisional. A model needs at least 30 implemented tasks spanning at least two assessed tiers with at least 5 tasks each to be ranked, and the board needs three such models. Models whose scores are not distinguishable share a band, cheapest first.

Use 'orbit agents rank calibrate' to freeze a new season's noise scales in the workspace's state; until then the scales are estimated from the ranked window.

Usage: orbit agents rank [OPTIONS]
       orbit agents rank <COMMAND>

Commands:
  calibrate  Freeze a new season's noise scales from the settled window

Options:
      --window <WINDOW>
          Time window, such as 7d or 30d [default: 30d]

      --until <WHEN>
          End before the settle lag: an RFC 3339 instant or a duration ago such as 7d [default: now]

      --settle-lag <DURATION>
          How long before --until the ranked window ends [default: 14d]

      --execution <SCOPE>
          Which tasks count by derived execution: managed, all or interactive [default: managed]

      --live
          Rank the window ending at --until, unsettled and provisional

  -h, --help
          Print help (see a summary with '-h')

Global options (see 'orbit --help' for descriptions):
  --root <ROOT>
  --workspace <SELECTOR>
  --format <MODE>
  --json
  --header
