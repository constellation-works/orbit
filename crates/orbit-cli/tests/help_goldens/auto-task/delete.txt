Delete a definition with its scheduler cursor and delivery consumer state; a deleted shipped default stays out of later reseeds

Usage: orbit auto-task delete [OPTIONS] <NAME>

Arguments:
  <NAME>
          Definition name

Options:
      --reason <REASON>
          Why the definition is deleted, kept in the audit record

      --force
          Delete even while a minted task is open or a delivery action is executing. The open tasks stay as they are

  -h, --help
          Print help (see a summary with '-h')

Global options (see 'orbit --help' for descriptions):
  --root <ROOT>
  --workspace <SELECTOR>
  --format <MODE>
  --json
  --header
