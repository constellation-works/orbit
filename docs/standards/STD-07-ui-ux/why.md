# Why — STD-07 UI and UX

Why each rule in [STD-07](STD-07.md) exists, tied to the corrections behind it. Task ids are Orbit tasks on dk-server-2. Session ids are the first 8 characters of a Claude Code transcript, and the owner's corrections are recorded as facts, with the words kept in the scribe entries and tasks. Read a rule's entry when its intent is unclear or before deviating. The rules themselves are in `STD-07.md`; this file explains them and adds none.

The rules come from two sweeps on 2026-11-09 (ORB-23557):
- about 95 UI fix tasks across ws_orbit, ws_orbit-office and ws_constellation-site;
- about 110 corrections across 331 interactive sessions, 2026-09-25 to 2026-11-09.

A rule is here only when its correction recurred.

## Principles

The principles (v15, ORB-26273) were distilled from the rule entries below, not written first. They exist because, in November 2026, the standard grew by about one rule a day (R38 to R44 and a refinement of R1, from 10 `standards` frictions), each written after the owner caught a case no rule covered. A principle lets a reviewer judge the next such case before he has to.

- **P1** gathers the most repeated theme: R16 (18 tasks, 9 sessions: the owner called the repeated detail noise and redundant) and R13 (8 sessions, 6 tasks: the owner found many long sentences chaotic).
- **P2** gathers text that appeared and shifted the whole panel up and down (R5), the collapse that showed Drain in place of Tasks, a page other than the one the owner was on (R43), and a select whose look changed with its behaviour (R37).
- **P3** gathers R1, the single most repeated correction (9 sessions), R2 (7 sessions, 7 tasks) and R27's preference for removing a tile over leaving one out on a row of its own.
- **P4** starts from the owner's question, after a fixed 330px width, of how an optimal width should be determined (R42). Orbit's dashboard showed what guessing costs: 25 font sizes, 27 radii, about 240 padding values and 158 hex literals outside its token file, with no spacing or type tokens to derive from (ORB-26273, 2026-11-21). Deriving colours from a few inputs follows Linear, which generates each theme from a base colour, an accent and a contrast level instead of 98 hand-set variables.
- **P5** gathers tables that did not use a window with enough width (R23), status pills on two rows when there was more space (R38) and an empty right column (R28). It is where this standard parts from Linear: Linear's calm comes from receding chrome and space, while the owner's corrections ask for density that fits.
- **P6** gathers a chart that put a second-ranked crew below the median (R19), a lone figure that did not belong alone in its panel (R29), and the owner's preference for a chart over raw data (R18).
- **P7** gathers R20 (6 sessions: the owner proposed a "repairing" pill for the state), the owner's finding that an idle state was invisible (R10), and a status change that did not collapse its item, asked twice (R22).
- **P8** gathers the four requests that corrections become checks (R26), and the redesigns that skipped the mock, which are the ones that came back (R25).

Left out on purpose, because no correction asks for them: Linear's dimmer, receding chrome, and keyboard-first navigation with a command menu. R44's "esc" labels are the only seed of the second.

## Layout and alignment

- **R1 (sibling spacing).** Raised in 9 separate sessions: the most repeated correction. The owner found the padding inconsistent for the "setup implement..." label compared to the other (cdd16d4b), an extra padding after "Approve" and "PR #5878" (6b0bb806), and too much unnecessary space in the left column (c43e4f47). Tasks: ORB-15181, ORB-17520. The symmetric-padding clause was added during review of v1 (2026-11-09, session 31048ad4). The task panel's title block had a tight top and a deep bottom under the title, so its top and bottom padding were inconsistent.
- **R2 (shared edges).** Raised in 7 sessions and 7 tasks: ORB-16937, ORB-15188, ORB-19331, ORB-21636, ORB-14442, ORB-19590, ORB-22614 (a 50:50 width). The owner asked that a panel be the same width as the "auto-drain" column, no more and no less (d5e3df3c), and that the height between the two columns be made consistent (ad7e58ba).
- **R3 (one column grid).** ORB-15180, ORB-17764, ORB-17900, ORB-17956, ORB-13839, ORB-16199: crew chips and row actions drifted between rows and groups.
- **R4 (stay inside the container).** ORB-19685, ORB-19908, ORB-12652, ORB-17294, ORB-15633, ORB-23172, plus sessions 49fa738d and 770f88b4 (overlapping and cut-off text).
- **R5 (no layout shift).** The owner reported that text appearing shifted the whole panel up and down (dbad1c64), and that the right-side column panel should not drop down along with the task board, asking for the "create task" section to become a popup window (ORB-20011). Also ORB-20022, ORB-22628 and ORB-23530.
- **R6 (peer controls).** ORB-23271 (a stepper like its sibling) and ORB-12889 (oversized selects). Changed in v18 (scribe standard-6): The owner corrected inconsistent button heights: heights must be uniform, widths should aim for uniformity to a lesser scrutiny, and the nearer buttons sit, or the more they share a function, the greater the imperative. In Water Economy (constellation-works/water-economy@ba6cfbd), four economy-table buttons measured 26, 40, 26 and 40px at 375px and 1440px because two labels wrapped onto a second line; shortening "Euro area" to "Euro" and "Rest of world" to "Rest" kept all four at 26px at the same font size while preserving full labels at 768px where they fit.
- **R7 (Close and Back top right).** The owner asked for "Back" to sit in the upper right corner (ORB-22754) and for the share-with-the-team toggle to sit next to the Close button, labelled just "Share" (ORB-21656); also ORB-17511.
- **R27 (no orphan tiles).** Added during review of v1 (2026-11-09, session 31048ad4). A stats row of six tiles wrapped "Hard" onto a row of its own, and the owner preferred removing one tile to leaving one out like that. This fits R2's shared edges and R16's "only what is acted on": a lone tile breaks the row's symmetry, and the choice that fixes it is usually to drop a tile, not to squeeze one in.
- **R28 (stacked blocks share the column width).** Added during review of v1 (2026-11-09, session 31048ad4). On constellation-site's Reviewers board, the provisional notice and the score explanation stopped at about half the page while the table ran the full width, leaving the right column empty. This is the vertical counterpart of R2's shared edges. A reading-width cap on prose is a good reason to cap a line's length, but it is no reason to leave half the page blank: the space goes to a related block, or the prose goes behind an info icon. The same correction on prose against tables: the owner asked for a consistent width between the paragraphs and tables (f2c1c565, 2026-09-27), repeated on the crew-rankings note on 2026-11-09 as inconsistent widths, where the table also clipped its last column (R4).

- **R30 (one look per kind of element).** Added during review of v1 (2026-11-09, session 31048ad4). The Orbit dashboard has a task lifecycle strip (Proposed → Done) and a run stage strip (Setup → Re-review). Their bars differ in height and weight, their top and bottom margins differ, and "Implement · 2m" wraps to two lines, with the two strips differing in height, boldness and top and bottom margin. Earlier: ORB-19326 and ORB-19336 (strips and severities styled per view), and ORB-19357 (completed is green on every strip). A component copied per view drifts apart, while a shared one can't. On the same strip a terminal step, "Awaiting approval", wrapped into a narrow column four lines tall, while its neighbours were truncated; the owner asked for every step to stay on a single line, with "Awaiting..." and a hover for the full text.

- **R31 (no needless nesting).** Added during review of v1 (2026-11-09, session 31048ad4). In the Orbit task panel, the "Execution summary" disclosure is a card, and its Markdown body is drawn in a second bordered card inside it, which the owner rejected as double nesting that draws no meaningful distinction. Every border costs padding on both sides and adds a level the eye has to resolve. Orbit's `ui.nested-card` rule already flags it.
- **R33 (metadata on its own line).** The owner asked for the metadata, such as "sol, sol@xhigh, sol@high", to sit in a new row (7995d040, ORB-24113): the Agents rank table ran each model's crews inline after its name, so long names and crew lists competed for one line.
- **R40 (inline list separators and icon spacing).** On the task detail's relation rows the owner asked what was wrong, then asked for less margin around ⓘ and some right margin after the comma, and then asked for it to be added to the standard (f09993e5, ORB-25033, F2026-11-303). Regression from rendered as `ORB-20629 ⓘ  ,ORB-17603 ⓘ`: the ⓘ's 44px tap target took up layout space, so the comma landed about 15px after the icon and touched the next id. Related to separated monospace ids with a bare sans space, so they read as run together. R1 covered gaps between siblings, but not separators inside one line or the space a tap target may add.
- **R47.** The owner's correction on the Orbit dashboard's Task detail comments (2026-11-21), recorded by agent fable as scribe standard-3: the last comment container stretched all the way down and should end where its comment ends, with consistent padding. The entry names R42 as nearest, but R42 covers a control's width only. R1 covers padding and sibling gaps, and P4 says sizes come from content without a height rule, so no rule today tells a reviewer that a stacked list's last item must not stretch.
- **R48.** Analytics > Metrics in the Orbit dashboard showed the same four containers (implementation time by actor, slowest runs, Actors within a complexity, Outcome by complexity) below the tab strip under every tab, so each tab mixed its own content with panels that belong to none of them. R16 (each piece of information once) is the nearest rule, but it does not say where content shared by several tabs sits relative to the strip; this rule does (scribe standard-4, ORB-28134).

The rule that monospace is for data only is Orbit's theme spec, which held up under these corrections.
- **R9 (label/value lists).** The same-row half was added during review of v1 (2026-11-09, session 31048ad4). On constellation-site's analytics pages, "Snapshot" sat on its own row above an indented "Latest snapshot …" line, so the label and its value were on different rows. ORB-20526 and ORB-20536. - **R35 (chart text on the type scale).** The owner found the texts inside the chart much bigger than the surrounding text and asked for similar sizes (7995d040, ORB-24114). The frontier chart drew a fixed 760-unit `viewBox` and stretched it to the panel, so its text grew with the panel.

## Colour and theme

- **R10 (sections and idle elements visible).** Raised in 6 sessions. The owner found an idle element needed more contrast against the background, as it was currently invisible (ca51ec07); asked for a card or container with a clear boundary or contrast between the detail section and the rest (dbad1c64); and asked for a different background colour on the metadata section for better contrast (31048ad4, ORB-23534). Also ORB-22757 and ORB-17763.
- **R11 (one meaning per colour).** White text on the incidents page and the friction Details panel was too bright compared with other pages (6f2baa40). Tasks: ORB-19357 (completed is green on every strip), ORB-17120, and ORB-16076 (pilot warnings as a yellow tag on the task).
- **R12 (declared default theme).** The default changed per surface without being written down. The owner asked for light mode, not dark mode, as the default (ORB-13354, September dashboard); the website then moved to dark (410a5432); the Orbit dashboard's current theme spec is dark. The rule makes each product state its choice once, instead of the standard picking one.
- **R45 (text colour follows role).** The owner, 2026-11-22, on a notice card in the Orbit dashboard whose title and four-line body were both the strongest text colour, found long text in `var(--fg)` uncomfortable to read and asked that `var(--fg)` be reserved for titles and labels for accentuation and `var(--fg-soft)` used for long text. The rule names roles instead of a length, because a one-line description is still prose and a long title is still a title; a length threshold would be chosen per view, which R11 forbids. On the dashboard's background the two steps measure about 16:1 and 12.5:1, so the softer one stays far above R10's 4.5:1. R11's own correction was the same complaint on one page: white text was brighter than on other pages.

## Words

- **R13 (minimal words).** The most repeated theme across tasks and sessions (8 sessions, 6 tasks), and the owner's stated standing preference. The owner asked that long summary lines and helper text be minimal in the UI (04aa5878); found many long sentences chaotic and suggested hover messages (dd5029a6); asked to delete the yellow warning texts and move them to hover text on a symbol (ORB-22789); and asked to cut taglines, helper text and secondary labels where not necessary (ORB-21727). Also ORB-22319, ORB-22326, ORB-22331 and ORB-22329.
- **R14 (one-line status, "show full", untruncated errors).** The owner found the "why stuck" text too verbose and asked for long texts to be truncated with a clickable "show full.." (895a3cbc), and for the acceptance criteria to get a "show full AC" button and default to collapsed (adc7f22c). Tasks: ORB-22321, ORB-18093, ORB-17505. The error exception comes from ORB-12978, ORB-14829 and ORB-19685, where truncation hid the cause an operator needed.
- **R15 (short labels without repeated context).** The owner asked for "View run" instead of "on mac-mini-1", since the host info is already present (6662e60f).
- **R16 (show each thing once, only what is acted on).** 18 tasks and 9 sessions. The owner found "537 denied" and "1 job" just noise (ORB-20662); questioned the point of displaying both, as redundant (dbad1c64); asked to hide Leases from the nav when disabled (1b5897bc); and proposed removing a page as somewhat redundant (ORB-21641). Also ORB-19252, ORB-19253, ORB-22598–22601, ORB-22627, ORB-19161, ORB-17276, ORB-20509, ORB-20502, ORB-22935, ORB-22755, ORB-22756 and ORB-19697. The drill-down half was added because the Fleet host dialog repeated the host card's load, memory, disk and I/O readings, and the owner asked to remove "health" because the previous page already includes it (2026-11-17, session orbit-0-orc, ORB-25399).
- **R32 (tooltips that add something).** The owner saw no need for hover help just to additionally display "done", since the green colour makes that obvious, wanting it only for what gets truncated, and found excess space in the hover container (7995d040, ORB-24124). Every progress-strip step had a tooltip, and each bubble was a fixed 280px wide. Changed in v18 (scribe standard-6): aligned with R41's narrow full-name exception (where shortening leads to ambiguity, provide hover help) so a tooltip revealing an ambiguously shortened button label is permitted while general button help tooltips remain forbidden. v13 replaced the closing sentence, which named a button's hover help (R41) as detail of this kind, when R41 was reversed (ORB-25398).
- **R41 (no hover help on buttons; effect in the accessible description).** The owner asked in session f09993e5 for a short hover help on every button saying what it does (F2026-11-304). The dashboard Residents tab's buttons (Start, Turn off, Wake for status, Pause…, Takeover…, Relay…, Stop…) gave no hint of their effect: whether Wake runs a cycle now, what Takeover hands over, or whether Stop can be undone. R32 limited tooltips to what the screen does not already show, which read as discouraging them on buttons. A button's effect is exactly that kind of off-screen detail. Changed in v18 (scribe standard-6): The owner followed up on shortened labels: where shortening leads to ambiguity, provide hover help. This carves out a narrow full-name-only exception—triggered only by ambiguous shortening, shown on hover, keyboard focus and touch, with the full accessible name preserved—distinct from general instructional button tooltips, which remain prohibited. Changed in v13: the owner reversed the hover help on buttons. On the Fleet page a button's help bubble stayed on screen after the pointer left, which the owner called a defect, and the owner asked to remove all hover-over help texts on that page except the info symbols, judging them redundant. The owner then asked for R41 itself to be amended (2026-11-17, session orbit-0-orc, ORB-25398). The effect text stays available to assistive technology, and explanations go behind info icons, as R13 already requires.
- **R46.** Daniel prefers unambiguous abbreviations, including scaled large numbers, to reduce UI verbosity while preserving meaning (scribe standard-7).

## Data display

- **R17 (answer first, collapse detail).** The owner asked that long tables or texts either paginate or have "show full" (cdd16d4b), and that long tables be a drop-down or similar (ORB-23343). The short-list half comes from the owner finding pagination in the routine view annoying and asking for no pagination to return (ORB-23384). The "no tabs for small sets" clause was added during review of v1 (2026-11-09, session 31048ad4). The exemption for a list that owns its region was added at the same review: a task's Context files tab lists all 33 selectors, and the owner said the rule does not apply to a list like this because it owns the rest of the bottom panels. Collapsing there would only add a click, because nothing else competes for the space. Also ORB-20525, ORB-22329, ORB-23480, ORB-23520 and ORB-19610.
- **R18 (visual over raw).** The owner asked for visuals like CPU and memory for backlog forecasts (ORB-20609), and for a chart like the dashboard's instead of raw data (31048ad4). Also ORB-23361, ORB-17931 and ORB-21605 (raw Markdown).
- **R19 (honest charts).** The owner found a chart made little sense, as the second-ranked crew sat below the median (52618a92, ORB-18538); asked that bars either be grouped by direction of better or all be aligned to the same direction (309ba5e1); and asked for something like a rolling sum (ORB-20003). Also ORB-23415 and ORB-23451.

- **R29 (summary with its detail).** Added during review of v1 (2026-11-09, session 31048ad4). On the Orbit dashboard's tool-call view, "Tool call failure rate · window 24h" was a full-width panel holding one line ("0.6% · 231 failed / 36626 tool calls") above the per-tool table it summarises, which the owner said does not belong there alone. - **R34 (chart labels by name).** The owner found the chart too cluttered and asked for details like "40.1 · $0.24 · 37 tasks" to be hover-over (7995d040, ORB-24120). Two-line captions on close points printed over each other and over the `fleet = 100` line.
- **R36 (tied groups in ranked order).** The owner asked that tied ranks be ordered by the score (7995d040, ORB-24118). The rank board ordered each tie band cheapest first, so scores inside a band jumped around. Cost-first was deliberate (a band means the scores can't be told apart), and the owner chose score order anyway, with cost still shown on each row.

## Responsiveness and interaction

- **R23 (use the width).** The owner asked for the screenshotted section to become a second column when the screen is wide enough (0c8aa357), and for tables to be fixed since the window had enough width (de43d4bd). The narrow half comes from ORB-20453, ORB-22756 (nav redundant beside the auto-drain panel), ORB-14442, ORB-23530 and ORB-23172.
- **R24 (reachable actions).** The owner asked for the "Comment" button to be sticky to the bottom, like the buttons in "overview" (75ceb1a9), and for the setting detail panel, which disappeared on scroll, to stay visible as the page scrolls (ad7e58ba).
- **R37 (behaviour change keeps the look).** The owner asked to revert the drop-down CSS to its original before the change (7995d040, ORB-24111). ORB-23882 made the rail workspace select multi-select by replacing the native control with a custom popup; ORB-24027 restored single-select but kept the new chrome and coloured options, which nobody had approved.
- **R38 (controls use the width before wrapping).** The owner asked that the task status pills use the entire remaining width rather than two rows when there is more space (f09993e5, ORB-24686, F2026-11-288). The Tasks status chips sat in the list column, so they wrapped after four chips while the space beside them, above the drain panel, stayed empty. R23 covered second columns and tables, not a row of controls. Version 4 also said a view-wide bar spans the view; the fix built from it (ORB-24686) gave the chips a row of their own at every width and pushed the drain panel's tabs down where the chips already fit their column. The owner corrected it: only in the range of screen widths where the status pills needlessly collapse to two rows while space is left to the right should they extend into the next columns (ORB-24777, F2026-11-294). Version 5 limits the rule to that range.
- **R39 (header and summary rows hold one line).** The owner asked that these parts stay on one line: where space is short, shrink some members, and where that is not enough, remove them in order of least importance (f09993e5, ORB-24812, F2026-11-296). At a narrow dock width the drain card's header (title, host select, state pill, time left) and its counts row each broke onto two lines. R14 covered a status line of text, R27 a lone orphan, R38 a row of controls with space beside it, and R23 said narrow widths drop secondary content without giving an order. Version 6 said members shrink "when the row does not fit" but not that they keep full size where it does. The Tasks dock tabs then shrank to 12px behind a fixed 440px container query (ORB-24810), which also covered the dock's normal width, where the 13.5px tabs fit. The owner corrected it: the tabs had become smaller than before, so they were to revert to their original size, shrink only when no space remains, and the UX rule was to be updated (f09993e5, ORB-25032, F2026-11-302). Version 7 keeps full size wherever the row fits, and names tab strips. Changed in v18 (scribe standard-6): when a row of buttons is constrained, shortening labels by content (such as "Euro area" to "Euro" and "Rest of world" to "Rest") preserves one line and uniform peer height without shrinking readable type, while full labels stay at widths where they fit (such as 768px in Water Economy).
- **R42 (controls sized by content).** The owner asked for the drain card's target select to be wider (ORB-25031), and the fix gave it a fixed `width: 330px`. Looking at the result, the owner asked how an optimal width should be determined (f09993e5, ORB-25074, F2026-11-310). A fixed width is a guess: it leaves an empty box for short names and clips long ones. R39 said how a row shrinks but not how wide a member starts. The owner approved sizing it from its longest option, capped by the row. Changed in v18 (scribe standard-6): connects content-sized button widths to R6's height-first uniformity and R39/R41's content-based shortening so Water Economy's four buttons keep comparable single-line 26px heights at 375px and 1440px with "Euro" and "Rest" while keeping full labels at 768px.
- **R43 (a collapse keeps the page you are on).** On the Orbit Tasks page, any click on a dock tab (Drain, Notices, Residents, Log) at two-column width marked the dock as the routed page, deliberately (ORB-19732). Narrowing the window then showed the Drain card in place of the task list. The owner reported that when the task and drain pages collapse into separate pages, the resulting page is Drain, and that it should be the Tasks page, since that was the page the owner was on (f09993e5, ORB-25093, F2026-11-311). R5 kept neighbours from shifting and R23 said narrow widths drop secondary content, but nothing said which region survives a collapse.
- **R44 (close buttons read "esc").** The owner, 2026-11-17, on the Orbit dashboard, asked that the close button of everything closable with Esc be labelled 'esc' instead of 'x', 'close' or 'back to list'. The owner had just learned that Esc closes the Tasks detail panel and preferred it to browser Back (ORB-25406, withdrawn). The label teaches the shortcut where the eye already goes to close (ORB-25389).

## Process

- **R25 (approved mock first).** Nine redesigns went through a design-canvas mock the owner approved before the build: ORB-21727, ORB-21345, ORB-20525, ORB-22329, ORB-23480, ORB-23520, ORB-17763, ORB-17933 and ORB-17934. The ones that skipped the mock are the ones that came back.
- **R26 (corrections become rules).** The owner has asked four times for the owner's UI corrections to become automated checks: cdd16d4b, 1b5897bc, 0d19f735, 04aa5878, and ORB-20526, ORB-20536, ORB-21637, ORB-20865. A correction that exists only in chat recurs, while a task is searchable, and the owner prefers to search tasks over transcripts. That is why every correction is filed as a task, and why this standard grows by searching UI fix tasks.
